# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a useful, calm place to learn and contribute,
regardless of diabetes status, body size, age, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity, experience level, nationality, race, religion or sexual orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be precise and kind. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, but correct misinformation with sources.
- Respect the medical boundary: no one here is your clinician, and contributors
  must not give individualized medical advice.
- Do not shame food choices, bodies or health outcomes. The product exists to
  redirect cravings without moralizing.
- Give credit and cite evidence when changing nutrition claims.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing or sexualized attention.
- Medical advice presented as a substitute for professional care.
- Deliberate misinformation about nutrition, diabetes or a contributor's work.
- Spam, sustained disruption or bad-faith argument.
- Publishing private information without consent.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any project
space, and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report a concern to the maintainer through GitHub (open an issue asking for a
private channel, or use GitHub's report tools). Reports will be reviewed as
quickly as possible and handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove
content and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban participation.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1
(<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>), adapted
for a health-education project.
